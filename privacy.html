Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Potential Damage is a free, fan-made League of Legends mini game. We collect as little as we can: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies, and the only analytics are anonymous page-view counts. This page explains exactly what is stored and why.

What stays in your browser

The game saves a few things in your browser's local storage so it works between visits. They never leave your device unless noted below, and you can erase them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

  • Your best score, today's Daily result and your chosen language.
  • The display name you last typed for the leaderboard.
  • A random player ID (for example 3f2a…) created the first time you play. It isn't linked to your name, email, Riot account or device, and it is only sent when you submit a score or open the leaderboard (see below).

What we store when you submit a score

Submitting to the leaderboard is optional. When you do, our server re-runs your draft and fight to verify the score and saves:

DataWhy
Display name you choose (up to 16 characters)Shown publicly on the leaderboard
Random player IDKeeps one entry per player and highlights your own row. Never shown to others.
Champion, items, score, game mode, date and patchShown publicly on the leaderboard
Time of submissionOrdering and abuse prevention

We don't store your IP address, email, Riot ID, location or any other personal information. Please don't use your real name or anything personal as a display name: it is public. Names that are hateful, harassing, sexual or that impersonate staff or Riot are rejected automatically and hidden from the boards.

Deletion runs automatically on the server. You can ask us to delete your entries sooner at any time (see Contact).

Abuse prevention

To stop spam and automated score farming, the server limits how often one visitor can start Free Play runs and submit scores. To do that it keeps a short-lived counter keyed by a one-way, secret-keyed hash of your IP address and the time window. The IP address itself is never stored, the hash can't be turned back into it, and counters are deleted within an hour.

Score submissions may also be protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, a privacy-friendly bot check that runs in your browser and is checked by our server (Turnstile privacy addendum).

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views, visits, referring sites, countries and device types. It uses no cookies or local storage, doesn't fingerprint your browser and doesn't follow you across sites. We only see totals, never individual visitors. We may share these totals (for example, monthly visits) with sponsors.

Riot Games data

Champion, item and ability data comes from Riot's public Data Dragon and the community-run CommunityDragon. To estimate in-game stacks (like Veigar or Cho'Gath), we use the Riot Games API once per patch to read a sample of high-elo ranked matches. Only aggregate numbers are kept (for example, the median stacks at 30 minutes per champion). We don't store or publish any player's name, Riot ID, PUUID or match history, and we don't use Riot data to identify anyone.

Services that handle your requests

Like any website, the services below receive your IP address and basic browser information when your browser contacts them. They process it under their own privacy policies; we don't receive or keep it.

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website (privacy statement).
  • Cloudflare runs the leaderboard server and database, the bot check and the page-view counts (privacy policy).
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts (privacy policy).
  • Riot Games Data Dragon and CommunityDragon serve game data and images.

Children

The game doesn't ask for personal information and isn't directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has entered personal information as a display name, contact us and we'll remove it.

Your choices

  • Play without submitting scores: nothing about you is sent to our server.
  • Clear this site's data in your browser to remove everything stored locally.
  • Ask us to delete or rename your leaderboard entries.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

For questions or deletion requests, open an issue at github.com/Nicksshoe/Nicksshoe.github.io/issues. For deletion, include your display name and roughly when you submitted.
